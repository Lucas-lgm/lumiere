Privacy

Lumiere is a local video player. Your files stay on your Mac.

No data collection — we don't track what you watch, when, or how often.
No analytics — no Google Analytics, no Mixpanel, no telemetry.
No network requests — Lumiere works fully offline. The only outbound connection is checking for updates (which you can disable).
No account required — no sign-up, no login, no cloud.
Your files never leave your Mac. We never see them.
